How we work

Three phases, one
accountable studio.

Phase 01 · Discovery
Brief, site, budget — locked.

We start with a site visit, a stakeholder interview, and a budget conversation. No design begins until scope, timeline and commercial terms are mutual.

  • Site survey & measure-up
  • Headcount & workflow audit
  • Budget & timeline locked
  • Scope of work signed
Phase 02 · Design
Two directions. One chosen.

We present two distinct design directions with 3D walkthroughs and material boards. You choose. We then develop the chosen direction into GFC drawings and a locked BOQ.

  • Concept & mood boards
  • 3D walkthroughs & renders
  • GFC drawings & BOQ
  • Material & vendor lock-in
Phase 03 · Build
On site, on schedule, on budget.

Our project manager is on site five days a week. You receive a daily report with photos. Snagging is done in two passes before handover, and we stay accountable for 12 months after.

  • Civil, MEP & finishes
  • Daily site reports
  • Two-pass snagging
  • 12-month warranty
The Studio

Commercial design
without the handoffs.

Virata is a commercial interior and exterior design studio. We work on corporate offices, retail, pharma, healthcare and hospitality projects across India — designing, documenting and building from a single contract.

Most commercial design problems are not aesthetic. They are coordination problems. We solve them by keeping design, documentation, procurement and execution under one roof, and one accountable principal.

"A commercial space is a tool. We tune the tool until your team forgets the building exists and gets on with the work."
